# Implementation and preservation status

Updated 16 November 2026. This records a working preview, not completion of the requested AAA reconstruction.

- 21 playable scenes: the traditional eighteen names plus Kalighat, Madurai and Tuljapur. Peetha identification traditions are not uniform; Prayag's current scene is explicitly Alopi rather than a claim to settle the Madhaveswari/Lalita identification.
- 133 mapped destinations include subsidiary shrines, sacred forms and historic monuments. The number is not a count of 133 authenticated murti models or an exhaustive temple inventory.
- Every destination has an identity and tradition field, with source notes on the map. Nearby titles are limited to three to reduce overlap; all destinations remain searchable.
- Original iconographic studies now cover Vishnu, Ganesha, Sarasvati, Varahi, Murugan, Annapurna and Surya. General museum references establish attributes, not the exact installed images. Sacred wells, footprints and Sosthi Tala's three stones have distinct non-figurative studies.
- The actual runtime artwork inventory is `current_art_inventory.json`. It records 34 reference altars whose installed images remain unbuilt, four reference locations (including Manikyamba and Jwala-associated objects), and existing anchors whose older artwork still needs individual review. Do not present all existing anchors as accurate sculptures.
- Draksharamam includes the two-level Bhimeswara sanctuary and accessible stairs. The separate Manikyamba image is unresolved. Jwalamukhi uses nine named flames, not nine goddess statues. Sharada represents roofless remains; no original idol or speculative roof is supplied.
- Pandua now represents the minaret and roofless Bari Masjid from ASI photographs and a site plan. The minaret is not labelled a surviving Shrinkhala temple tower. Exact ancient shrine identification remains unresolved. Interior spiral stair access is not modelled.
- Five original guide clips were generated using the existing configured project voice. They are explanatory narration, not claimed historical mantras. The selected shrine panel offers playback where a recording exists. Existing chants remain available.

## Epigraphy and building sculptures

The earlier builders did not contain authenticated inscription transcriptions or an object-by-object record of building sculptures. Their decorative geometry cannot be called historical preservation. Existing geometry and the original Gemini baseline have been retained locally; the hypothetical Pandua temple was superseded by the current-monument study.

No invented ancient script, edict or guessed inscription has been added. Accurate preservation still requires primary close-up photographs, readable transcription, language/date attribution and physical placement for each inscription. Carvings similarly require matched views, positions and condition records. These requirements remain outstanding across the collection. The in-game Architecture guide discloses this gap.

Sources and photographic comparisons for the new sites are listed in `present_day_sites/README.md`. Pandua's primary sources are the ASI Kolkata Circle entries for [The Minar](https://asikolkata.in/monument-sites/the-minar/) and [The Mosque](https://asikolkata.in/monument-sites/the-mosque/). Source images remain research references, not copied game textures.
