# Rejected and conflicting reference leads

- Meenakshi temple plan from Pearland, Texas: wrong site for Madurai. Search results returned it; excluded.
- Samalkota Kumararama aerial labelled near Draksharamam results: different Pancharama temple; excluded.
- Pithapuram pages calling Kukkuteswara one of the five Pancharamas: factual identity warning; not used as authority.
- Search results for Mangala Gauri at Varanasi: different temple from the Gaya Peetha; excluded.
- Sri Sringeri Sharada Peetham, Pilani Sharda temple and modern Teetwal Sharada temple: different sites from the Neelum Valley ruins; excluded as reconstruction references.
- Jwala Ji at Khrew, Kashmir: different from Kangra Jwalamukhi; excluded.
- Attractive generic/stock Srisailam imagery beside a broad river: site match unresolved; do not accept from caption alone.
- AI-labelled Pithapuram devotional videos: excluded as geometry or iconography evidence.
- Kamakhya chamber counts: authority uses five named chambers; Assam government account uses four. Reconcile terminology against a plan rather than deleting a hall to match a count.
- Biraja lion: trust iconography says absent; district tourism description includes a lion. Preserve disagreement and consult an authenticated image before sculpting.
- Ujjain Mahakali: stotram name does not uniquely establish that Harsiddhi, Gadkalika and Mahakaleshwar are one site. This dossier records Harsiddhi as the game's chosen site.
- Prayaga Madhaveswari: Alopi and Lalita Devi are distinct modern sites; avoid treating alternate traditional identifications as a single compound.
- National Sanskrit University source: stotram pages list the eighteen, but later tabular slides add a Danteswari row. Use the verse for membership and do not import body-part labels as settled fact.
- Kolhapur tourism page: conflicting solar dates appear within the same page. No astronomical alignment dates have been adopted.
- Tuljapur trust DPR: includes development proposals and town-wide water features. A proposal is not proof of an existing feature or a courtyard location.

All retained aerial and photographic references are for inspection. Creator rights and actual recording dates must be checked before redistributing media or converting imagery into game assets.
