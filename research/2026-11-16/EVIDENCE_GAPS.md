# Evidence still required before exact reconstruction

All 21 destinations have architecture/identity notes and source links. None has been certified as an exhaustive modern shrine survey. The CSV contains named shrines AND halls, water features, images, ritual objects and regional sites; its row count is not a count of separate temples.

For each living temple, obtain:

1. A dated, legible north-marked ground plan with scale, identifying the exact campus boundary.
2. A current overhead reference and two oblique exterior views, matched by gateways/tanks rather than caption alone.
3. A continuous approved ground walkthrough identifying each shrine, doorway and floor level; satellite roofs cannot reveal these.
4. An iconography reference for the actual main sacred form; festival ornamentation may obscure it.
5. A distinction between permanent fabric, temporary queue/festival structures, and proposed redevelopment.
6. A complete inventory signed off against temple-authority documentation or on-site survey. Published short tourist lists are not exhaustive.

- Kanchi: avoid mixing present Kanchi shrine features with Bangaru Kamakshi at Thanjavur.
- Tuljapur: trust DPR is indexed but direct fetch returned 403; existing/proposed drawings remain to be separated.
- Madurai: locate a readable north-marked plan; reject Pearland, Texas plans. Satellite views clarify the roof density but cannot identify every internal shrine.
