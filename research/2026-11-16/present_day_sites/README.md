# Present-condition scene studies

These three scenes fill the previously missing traditional destinations. They are original procedural studies, not photogrammetry or surveyed digital preservation.

## Evidence used

- Draksharama: [APSRTC government description](https://apsrtc.ap.gov.in/fp_draksharamam.php) documents two prakaras, four gateways, five-tier western gopuram, two upper-sanctum stairs and named associated shrines. A [published courtyard photograph](https://hindutemples-india.blogspot.com/2019/04/bhimeshwara-swamy-temple-draksharamam-temple.html) was inspected for dark stone massing, whitewashed upper tower, mandapa and brass flagstaff. The installed Manikyamba image has not been authenticated, so the reference location is not labelled an exact sculpture.
- Jwalamukhi: [Kangra temple authority](https://kangratemples.hp.gov.in/shri-jawala-mata-mandir/) documents natural-flame worship and nine traditional identities. [Himachal Tourism](https://himachaltourism.gov.in/destination/kangra/) describes the gilded dome. A [published courtyard photo](https://hindutemples-india.blogspot.com/2019/12/jwalamukhi-devi-temple-kangra-himachal-pradesh.html) was inspected for the arcaded white hall, bulbous golden dome and checker paving. The nine flame locations and subsidiary object shapes are schematic, not authenticated close-up replicas.
- Sharada: [Department of Archaeology and Museums](https://doam.gov.pk/public/sites/6633) records the surviving sanctuary, enclosure, 63 stone-slab steps, square pillars, recessed motifs and absent roof. Photographs in [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1751139), a [first-person Local Guides contribution](https://www.localguidesconnect.com/t/history-of-sharda-university-neelum-valley-azad-kashmir/240625), and [a published interior view](https://swarajyamag.com/culture/a-goddess-beyond-access-the-sharada-peetha-that-lies-across-the-loc) were inspected. No roof, original murti, exact epigraphy or conjectural ancient subsidiary shrine is supplied.

## Limits

Architecture dimensions, relative positions, surface colours and damaged-stone patterns remain approximate. The Sharada step count is sourced; its rise and tread are gameplay dimensions. Reference photos are research-only local files, not game textures, and no reuse permission is assumed. No inscription has been invented to fill an empty wall. The game must not describe these scenes as exact present-day replicas, complete conservation records, or AAA art.
